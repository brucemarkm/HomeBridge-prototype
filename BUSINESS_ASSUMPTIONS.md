# Business assumptions

- Proposed buyer-representation fee: **$2,995 per completed transaction**, configurable in `lib/config.ts` and locally in the administrator demo.
- Buyer-first launch concept in **Utah County**: Provo, Orem, Springville, Spanish Fork, American Fork, Lehi, and Pleasant Grove.
- Technology simplifies discovery, organization, and repetitive work. Real licensed professionals would handle representation, negotiations, contract review, material decisions, and official transaction confirmations.
- Homebridge is not currently an operational licensed brokerage. The prototype establishes no client/professional relationship.
- Comparison commission defaults to 2.5% solely as an editable example, not a standard or required market rate. All compensation is negotiable.
- Fee differences are not automatically buyer savings. Seller-paid compensation and concessions depend on agreements and lender rules. No cash rebate or concession eligibility is promised.
- Administrator examples model a $1,250 transaction cost and 42-day cycle for demonstration. Neither is verified. Revenue potential is not earned revenue; fixed costs, taxes, marketing, and other expenses are excluded.
- Mortgage defaults (6.5%, 30 years, 20% down, illustrative taxes/insurance) are not live rates, loan offers, or approvals. Mortgage insurance is simplified.
- All listings, addresses, prices, comparable examples, histories, agents, transactions, and business results are fictional. Reused generated architectural imagery does not depict the fictional properties.
- Future seller expansion and additional geography are hypotheses that require legal, operational, and economic validation.
