# Homebridge business assumptions

- Buyer-only initial beta, focused on Utah County with provider contracts that can expand to other markets.
- Proposed **$2,995 flat buyer representation fee**, subject to future licensing, service design, and brokerage arrangements. It is not a legally operative offering or guaranteed saving.
- AI/automation support research, education, information gathering, financial exploration, and administrative preparation. Properly licensed professionals would handle actual representation, strategy, negotiations, approved agreements, and official transaction oversight.
- Compensation is negotiable. The percentage-based comparison is an editable illustration, not a customary or required rate. Seller contributions and lender-permitted concessions vary; the fee difference is not automatically buyer cash savings, credit, or refund.
- Four fictional scenarios model distinct buyer questions: condo HOA/reserves/access, townhome cooling/rules, starter-house moisture/roof history, and larger-home costs/drainage. These are not active listings, actual disclosures, verified comparables, or real seller statements.
- Mortgage defaults are illustrative, editable assumptions, not live quotes or lender approvals. Known-data tax/HOA values remain explicitly fictional/user-supplied/provider-reported. Model cash needs exclude unresolved actual credits, deposits, prepaids, lender/title adjustments, and proposed representation fee.
- No real licensed professional is assigned or contacted in demo review. A simulated completion is not legal approval, contract execution, or an offer submission.
- Seller-specific functionality is intentionally excluded. Real services and expanded geography require legal, licensing, operational, security, and economic validation.
